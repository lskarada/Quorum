## TL;DR
DxGPT is Microsoft's rare-disease diagnostic decision-support tool, positioned as a companion to MAI-DxO that focuses on the long tail of diagnostic "zebras" rather than the common-case differential. It is designed to help clinicians shorten the diagnostic odyssey for rare-disease patients by surfacing low-prevalence conditions consistent with a symptom profile.

## Key claim
A dedicated LLM-backed diagnostic assistant focused on rare diseases can help clinicians shorten the time-to-diagnosis for low-prevalence conditions that are typically missed by routine differentials.

## Relevance to Quorum
DxGPT is the rare-disease counterpart to MAI-DxO in Microsoft's diagnostic-AI lineup. Quorum's scope is deliberately the common-case differential — the volume cases where panel deliberation, calibrated confidence, and audit transparency matter most. Citing DxGPT lets us (a) demonstrate awareness of Microsoft's full diagnostic-AI surface, not just the headline MAI-DxO paper, and (b) explicitly delimit Quorum's scope: we do not claim the rare-disease zebra-hunter role, and a production deployment would route candidate zebras to a tool like DxGPT rather than try to absorb that workload into the panel.
